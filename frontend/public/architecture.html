Mirror Engine · Delta Exchange India

How the copier works

A master trades. Every follower gets the same trade, scaled to its own balance, in under a second. A second system then checks the result every ten seconds and fixes what the first one got wrong.

01The shape

Everything runs on one EC2 box under Docker Compose — FastAPI backend, Redis, Next.js dashboard — with Supabase as the database and a copy of the dashboard on Vercel.

An owner holds one or more groups; a group is one master and its followers. One owner can run several masters at once, which is why almost every piece of the engine's state has to be keyed per master rather than per owner.

02Sizing

# auto_ratio — the mode every live group runs ratio = follower_balance / master_balance qty = master_quantity × ratio # JD group, logged 2026-11-19 22:05 UTC 7595.09 / 8509.61 = 0.892531 master 800 lots → follower 715 lots

Total balance, not free margin. Free margin shrinks as positions lock it up, so a ratio built on it swings with the book — on 2026-08-03 the same unchanged positions read "expected 22" and then "expected 30" half an hour later, firing four false alerts.

An unreadable balance returns zero, never 1:1. On 2026-08-02 a master's balance read as 0.0 five times in a day; a 1:1 fallback would have sized a 610-lot target for a 70 USD account. A missed copy is recoverable, a 100× order is not.

03What the reconciler does

Every 10 seconds, per leg
Follower is flatFollower holds
Master is flat nothing to do CLOSE
Master holds OPEN TRIM / TOP-UP
to the ratio target

It will not act when

seen onceConfirm on the next pass first. A book read mid-flight looks wrong for one pass and then isn't
gap < 5%Or under 1 lot. A 1-lot gap on a 30-lot leg is noise; acting on it churns
book unreadableA failed read is never permission to act — except for a close, where refusing would leave a position the master has exited
no ratioTarget reads 0, and trimming on that would close the whole position
master unwindingHe has a working order reducing his own position. Trimming into it fills at a different price than his (desk call, 25 Sept)
follower buildingA resting order would still ADD to the position, so the size isn't settled
his stop hitHands-off — the follower's own jittered stop closes that leg
entry > 180s oldA missed leg is not chased once the price has moved on. It alerts instead
master below peakHe has net-reduced on this leg; he is unwinding, not re-entering. No top-up

Last look. The size is re-read fresh immediately before the order goes out, and the engine sends the smaller of what it planned and what is true now. Under-correcting is picked up ten seconds later; over-correcting is a real trade in the wrong direction.

04The clock

Why 10s, and not 5
We tried 5s. Measured over the 14 hours either side, order-mirror latency went p90 0.03s → 0.22s and max 1.34s → 5.35s. 5.35s touches Delta's ~5s signature window — the same event-loop pressure that had orders rejected as expired_signature in July. 10s is half the confirmation time of the old 15s for a fraction of the added load.

05What it tells you

Telegram, routed to each owner's own group
🔴 Position OpenedA trade was copied
✅ Position ClosedA position was closed
⚠️ MismatchPosition size, a missing leg, a missing TP/SL, a stop the master dropped, or a resting order the follower never got — within 10–20s
🔧 Reconciler correctedWhat it changed, and why
⏸ Holding on purposeA deliberate hold, so it is not mistaken for a broken one
⏳ Not corrected60s on, still wrong. Someone should look
⚠️ Mirror FailedThe exchange rejected an order
🔥 Engine errorThe engine crashed on its own correction. This leg will not be fixed
🛑 Stopped retryingAn order was placed three times and vanished each time. Nothing more happens automatically

One message per episode, not one per pass. The throttle clears when the problem settles, so the next occurrence speaks again.

06Where it is weak

Open · needs a decision Two systems can trade one account The engine assumes it is the only thing writing to a follower — that is what lets it treat any difference from the master as an error worth correcting. On 20 Nov an owner traded a follower directly; the engine saw four positions it had never opened, unprotected, on strikes the master was flat on, and would have market-closed about 8,000 lots had a crash not stopped it first.
Open · on hold Escalation is a flat timer A limit still filling at 5s is killed anyway, and the reconciler would market the remainder 15s later regardless. Both should gate on "our order is live and still filling", with a ceiling around 30s.
Open · unaudited 26 variables that might not be bound Across the two hottest functions. Two of them took the reconciler down on 20 Nov. The rest have been read and judged safe, but not proven — and reading is exactly what missed the first two.
Fixed · 20 Nov An alert line that crashed the reconciler Two variables read before they were assigned took down the whole correction pass; one of them had missing-leg recovery switched off in production for two days. Each leg now has its own guard, and a crashed correction alerts.

The pattern behind most of these. The engine is good at detecting that something is wrong and was historically bad at telling anyone when its own correction failed. Detection and correction live in the same process, so when correction breaks, detection usually goes quiet with it.
